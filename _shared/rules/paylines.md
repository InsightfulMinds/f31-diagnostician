# Paylines and the pass/fail threshold (canonical home)

## Threshold rule

A submission PASSES when its percentile is at or inside the assigned
IC's published payline for the fiscal year and activity code. Outside
the payline is the failure this workspace explains. Cohort and threshold
follow recommendation.md and are PENDING Sean's confirmation at the
packaging step.

## How scores become a payline decision

1. Each assigned reviewer scores F1 and F2 on the 1 to 9 scale (1 best)
   and rates F3 as appropriate or gaps_flagged.
2. Discussed applications receive an overall impact score, 10 to 90
   (10 best), the average of panel member scores times 10.
3. The impact score converts to a percentile against the study section's
   recent base. The IC funds inside its payline.
4. Typical IC fellowship paylines have run in the 15th to 25th percentile
   band; the live figure is looked up per IC and FY at intake (question 6
   of `setup/questionnaire.md`). This file never hardcodes a live payline.
